The Book Owl

Bukioti means book owl — and every detail tells that story

Tucked on quiet Gelati Street in the heart of Kutaisi, Bukioti is more than a café. It's a deep-green, book-lined retreat where colorful tables, painted cloths, and shelves of Georgian wine and tea invite you to slow down.

Mother and daughter welcome you like family — with smiles, thoughtful recommendations, and portions made with unmistakable love. Read by the big street-facing window, swap a book from the shelf, or play chess on a rainy afternoon.

  • Best coffee in Kutaisi Cappuccino with proper foam, affogato, cold coffee with ice cream
  • Legendary desserts Lava cake guests call life-changing · Belgian waffles · apple dessert
  • Work & unwind Quiet enough to focus · cozy enough to never want to leave
